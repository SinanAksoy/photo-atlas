Map provider & Google Maps settings

The map starts with OpenStreetMap (no key). For Google Maps, enter a Google Maps JavaScript API key with billing enabled and the site origin authorized. Keys in client-side sites are public; restrict them by HTTP referrer.

Privacy: images and locations are processed in your browser and saved locally on this device. Map tiles and map API calls contact their providers. Use Backup to transfer your collection. Large images may exceed browser storage quotas.
